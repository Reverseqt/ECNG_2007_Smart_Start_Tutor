Part 1

The common gate family

AND, OR, NAND, NOR, buffer and inverter are the symbols in routine use. XOR and XNOR are included with them because both appear in the comparators and adders treated later.

AND gate

De Morgan relationships

X′ + Y′ = (X · Y)′ and X′ · Y′ = (X + Y)′.

These identities explain why NAND and NOR functions can be represented using alternative gate symbols with inverted inputs or outputs.

XOR is not a basic gate. It is built from the others: X ⊕ Y = X′·Y + X·Y′. It earns its own symbol because "the inputs differ" is needed so often — it is the sum bit of an adder and the not-equal test in a comparator.
