Signal Flow Through a Circuit

Circuits are formed by connecting gates in cascade. This activity introduces the internal node and the evaluation of a circuit in stages.

Part 1

Follow a signal through two gates

Practical circuits connect gates in cascade, the output of one forming the input of the next.

This circuit has three inputs. X and Y meet at an AND gate, whose output is the internal node N1. That node and W meet at an OR gate to produce Z. Set the switches and watch which wires carry a 1.

Part 3

Read the circuit as an expression

Grouping is significant: the same gates in a different order realise a different function.

Which expression describes the circuit in Part 1?
If W is held at 1, what is Z for every value of X and Y?
Hint

Set W to 1 in Part 1 and try all four combinations of X and Y. An OR gate with one input already at 1 cannot produce 0.

Answer both questions, then check your work.
