How to use this tutor

Every activity follows the same rhythm. First you explore — set switches, change inputs and watch the circuit respond. Then you predict an output or complete a table, and check your answer. Hints are there when you want them, and an activity is marked complete once you get the exercise right.

Progress is kept in this browser on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no sign-in.
