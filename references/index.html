Standards

Primary source

A note on device data

The voltage and current figures quoted throughout this tutor are representative values for the families concerned, chosen to illustrate the principles under discussion. They are not a substitute for a manufacturer's data sheet and should not be relied upon when designing or constructing a circuit around a specific part.
