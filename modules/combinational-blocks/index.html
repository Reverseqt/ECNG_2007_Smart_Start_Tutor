What this module covers

The blocks treated here route and translate bit patterns. None requires any interpretation of those patterns as numbers, characters or other quantities. Circuits that do depend on such an interpretation are treated later, in Arithmetic and Comparison.

  • Decoders and enable inputs
  • Encoders and priority encoding
  • Multiplexers as data selectors
  • Demultiplexers as data routers
  • Decoder outputs as minterms
  • Cascading decoders
  • Function synthesis with a multiplexer
