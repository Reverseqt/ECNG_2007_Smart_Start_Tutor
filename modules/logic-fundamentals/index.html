What this module covers

Gates, the correspondence between a gate, its Boolean expression and its truth table, and the behaviour of these in combination.

  • Inputs, outputs and signal flow
  • AND, OR and NOT operations
  • NAND and NOR
  • Buffer and inverter symbols
  • Gate truth tables
  • Predicting outputs
  • Reading simple Boolean expressions
  • De Morgan gate relationships
