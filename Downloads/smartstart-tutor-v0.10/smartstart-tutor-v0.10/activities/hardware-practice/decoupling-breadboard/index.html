Part 1

Why decouple an IC?

Wire inductance and rapid transistor switching together disturb the voltage seen at an integrated circuit's supply pin.

Without local charge storage, switching spikes and dips reach the supply pin more strongly.

A decoupling capacitor is placed close to the IC between power and ground, providing a local reservoir of charge.
