Part 2

Remove a static-1 hazard

For F = A′B + AC, hold B = C = 1 and change A. The steady-state output should remain 1, but unequal path delays can produce a glitch.

Consensus term BC
Add the common product term that bridges the adjacent minterms.
F = A′B + AC

The added product term contains neither A nor A′. It remains 1 throughout the transition and prevents the delay from propagating to the OR output.

Hazard presentThe output may dip to 0.
