Part 1

Operators and definitions

A dot denotes AND, a plus sign denotes OR, and a prime mark denotes NOT, or complement.

X · YAND: both conditions must be 1.
X + YOR: at least one condition must be 1.
X′NOT: the complement of X.

Axiom and theorem

Axiom or postulate: a basic statement accepted as true within the Boolean algebraic system.

Theorem: a statement that can be proved from the axioms and earlier results.

Part 3

The principle of duality

If a Boolean identity is true, its dual is also true. Form the dual by interchanging AND with OR and 0 with 1. Variables and complements do not change.

0↔1
