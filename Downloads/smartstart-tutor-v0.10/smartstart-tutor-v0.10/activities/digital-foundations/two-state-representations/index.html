Part 1

Two states can have many physical meanings

The symbols 0 and 1 do not always mean voltage.

Examples drawn from mechanics, memory, optics and storage show that any two distinguishable physical states can represent one bit. Step through the technologies and compare what each one uses for 0 and for 1. Voltage levels are a special case, handled in Analogue and Digital Values.

HIGH / 1

Part 2

Check your understanding

Decide which logic state each physical condition represents.

  • Fibre optics: light on
  • Relay logic: circuit open
  • Dynamic memory: capacitor charged
  • Read-only compact disc: no pit
  • Non-volatile memory: fuse intact
