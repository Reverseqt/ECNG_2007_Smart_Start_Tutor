Logic Levels and Noise Margins

The difference between the voltages a driver guarantees and those a receiver requires determines how much noise a connection can tolerate.

Part 1

Classify an input voltage

Every input divides its supply range into a guaranteed LOW region, an invalid region and a guaranteed HIGH region.

The preceding activities established the three bands qualitatively. Here they are given numerical limits, and the consequences of connecting two devices with different limits are examined.

Part 2

Check logic-family compatibility

Positive noise margins mean the guaranteed output levels satisfy the receiving input limits.

A driving output guarantees certain voltages; a receiving input requires certain voltages. The difference between the two is the noise margin: the interference the connection can absorb before a valid level ceases to be read correctly.
