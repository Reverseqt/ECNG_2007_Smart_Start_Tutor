7. Arithmetic and Comparison

Combinational circuits whose behaviour depends on the interpretation placed on their inputs. The module proceeds from display decoding and magnitude comparison through addition and subtraction, and concludes by deriving the comparator afresh and assembling an arithmetic and logic unit.

What this module covers

The circuits treated here interpret their inputs as numbers. Establishing that 1011 exceeds 0111 requires both operands to be read as positional binary values, and a seven-segment decoder is defined only once BCD has been. Each therefore depends on the representations established in Data Representation.

  • Hexadecimal seven-segment extension
  • Subtraction as addition of a complement
  • Borrow signalling
  • Comparison by subtraction
  • BCD as a display input code
  • Seven-segment mapping
  • Magnitude comparison of binary words
  • Comparing most-significant bits first
  • Half and full adders
  • Ripple carry and overflow
  • Two’s complement subtraction
  • Function selection with a multiplexer
