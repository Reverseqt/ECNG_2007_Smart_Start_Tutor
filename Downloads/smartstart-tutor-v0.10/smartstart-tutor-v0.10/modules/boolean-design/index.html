What this module covers

Boolean notation and the axioms and theorems of the algebra, the canonical forms, Karnaugh-map minimisation, static hazards, Petrick’s method, and the synthesis of a minimised circuit.

  • Boolean notation, axioms and theorems
  • Principle of duality
  • Compound truth-table construction
  • Minterms, maxterms, SOP and POS
  • K-map representation and adjacency
  • Prime and essential prime implicants
  • Don't-care conditions
  • Static-0 and static-1 hazards
  • Hazard-free SOP minimisation
  • Petrick’s method and cover cost
