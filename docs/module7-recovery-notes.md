# Module 7 recovery notes

Module 7 reconstructs the original **Integrated Circuit Technology** chapter as **Digital Hardware Practice**. The revised title reflects its position in the pedagogical sequence, but the content remains based on the FLA.

## Recovered material

- definition and classifications of integrated circuits;
- TTL/CMOS transistor technologies, package choices, and historical SSI–ULSI categories;
- input/output voltage and current terminology;
- DC noise margins, fan-in and fanout;
- MOSFET operation, CMOS inverter, NAND and NOR networks;
- diode and diode-element review;
- the original TTL/CMOS comparison categories and representative 5 V thresholds;
- floating-input behaviour and two remedies;
- bus contention, open-drain/open-collector outputs, and tri-state logic;
- the recovered open-collector truth table and tri-state OR-gate ActionScript;
- decoupling capacitors and the 7447 breadboard sequence;
- the original six breadboard tips;
- Schmitt-trigger hysteresis;
- free-wheeling diodes;
- LED series-resistor calculation.

## Modifications

- Flash animations and bitmap diagrams were replaced with HTML, CSS, SVG-style graphics, and JavaScript controls.
- Obsolete or broken external links were not embedded.
- Historical integration-scale classifications are labelled as such.
- Electrical values are presented as the representative values used by the original tutor; students are reminded to use current data sheets for real devices.
- Accessibility, touch interaction, responsive layout, and completion tracking were added.
